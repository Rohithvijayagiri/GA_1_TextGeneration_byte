At 12:47 a.m., the robot stopped moving. Maya stared at the terminal as a single red message appeared: MOTOR_TIMEOUT. She checked the wiring diagram, then the sensor logs. The encoder values were changing, but the controller believed the motor was frozen. After tracing the data path, she found the problem: a timestamp conversion was overflowing. One corrected line later, the robot moved its arm again. Maya smiled, committed the fix, and wrote a test so the bug could not quietly return. The lab was silent again, but the robot was finally doing exactly what the code told it to do.
