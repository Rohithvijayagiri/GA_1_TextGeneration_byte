A transformer processes sequences primarily through attention mechanisms, allowing each token to consider other relevant tokens in the sequence. Traditional recurrent neural networks process sequences step by step, maintaining a hidden state as they move through the input. This sequential structure can make long-range dependencies and parallel training more difficult. Transformers can process many tokens in parallel during training and use self-attention to model relationships between distant parts of the sequence. This architecture has become a foundation for many modern language and multimodal models.
