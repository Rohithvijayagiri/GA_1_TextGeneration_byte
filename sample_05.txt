Overfitting happens when a machine-learning model learns the training examples too closely instead of learning patterns that generalize. Imagine a student who memorizes the exact answers to ten practice questions. The student may score perfectly on those questions but struggle when the teacher changes the wording. A well-generalized model is more like a student who understands the underlying concept and can solve new problems. Common ways to reduce overfitting include using more data, simplifying the model, applying regularization, using dropout in neural networks, and evaluating performance on data that was not used for training.
