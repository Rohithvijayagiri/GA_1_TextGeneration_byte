BLE is generally designed for low-power, short-range communication and is well suited to battery-powered wearables that send small amounts of data. Wi-Fi usually provides higher throughput and can support heavier network traffic, but it typically consumes more power. For a fitness band that periodically sends heart-rate or activity data, BLE is often a practical choice. A wearable that needs continuous high-bandwidth communication, such as streaming large media files, may need Wi-Fi instead. The final choice depends on range, throughput, power budget, latency, and device requirements.
