I’m excited to share that I completed a Generative AI project focused on text generation using a statistical baseline. The project helped me understand how prompts, generated outputs, evaluation, and reproducibility fit together. I also compared the strengths and limitations of traditional probabilistic generation with modern neural approaches. The biggest lesson was that building an AI system is more than getting an output—it also means documenting the method, testing failure cases, and making the work reproducible. Looking forward to applying these fundamentals to larger AI projects.
