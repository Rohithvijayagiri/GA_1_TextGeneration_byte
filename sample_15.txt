The vibration sensor on Line 4 crossed its warning threshold. Instead of waiting for the central server, the edge controller compared the signal against a small anomaly model running locally. It detected a pattern that had appeared before a bearing failure. The controller slowed the machine and sent an alert to the maintenance team. Engineers inspected the bearing and replaced it before the line stopped. The event showed why combining IoT sensors with local intelligence can be valuable: the system could react immediately while still reporting the evidence to the central monitoring platform.
