AI can support electronics engineering in several practical ways. First, machine-learning models can detect abnormal patterns in sensor data and help predict equipment failures. Second, computer vision can inspect circuit boards for missing components, solder defects, or alignment problems. Third, optimization algorithms can help tune parameters in communication, power, or control systems. AI does not replace the underlying electronics knowledge: engineers still need to understand signals, components, constraints, and safety. Instead, AI can reduce repetitive analysis and help engineers make faster, data-driven decisions.
