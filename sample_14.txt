Edge computing moves some processing closer to where data is produced instead of sending every operation to a distant cloud service. This can reduce latency, lower bandwidth use, and keep certain data local. For applications such as industrial control, robotics, and real-time monitoring, waiting for a remote server may be too slow or unreliable. An edge device can perform immediate filtering, detection, or control while sending selected summaries to the cloud. The trade-off is that edge hardware has limited compute, storage, and power compared with large cloud infrastructure.
