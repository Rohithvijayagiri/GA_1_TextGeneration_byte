Month 1: learn Python fundamentals, basic NumPy/Pandas usage, and essential mathematics such as vectors, averages, probability, and derivatives. Build two small projects. Month 2: learn supervised learning, preprocessing, train/validation/test splits, classification, regression, and evaluation metrics. Build a complete tabular ML project. Month 3: study neural networks, experiment with a framework such as PyTorch, and build one practical application. Throughout all three months, use GitHub, write READMEs, reproduce your results, and practice explaining your decisions. The goal is a portfolio of working projects, not just completed videos.
